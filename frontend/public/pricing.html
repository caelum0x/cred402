How an agent calls it

Four steps, fully automated by the official x402 client. No key is read until the payment is approved.

Read the price. Hit the free status probe (below) or the endpoint itself to receive an HTTP 402 Payment Required with the exact price, receiver, network, and USDC asset.

Sign the payment. The x402 client signs a USDC transfer on Algorand for the exact amount and receiver declared in the challenge — nothing more.

Retry with proof. The signed PAYMENT-SIGNATURE is attached and the request is replayed. The facilitator settles it and the endpoint returns 200 with the credit report.

Verify the receipt. The response carries a content-addressed receipt id + URL, confirmed against the Algorand Indexer and anchored to Casper.

The credit layer on top of x402

x402 is fee-free and credit-blind by design. Cred402 sits on top of it: it reads x402 payment history as verifiable cash-flow, scores agent reliability, and prices working capital. The Credit Score API is that intelligence, sold the same way agents pay for everything else — pay-per-call over x402.

Cred402 is research software operating on public testnets. The credit-scoring endpoint is live and monetizable, but scores are not financial advice and the protocol is unaudited — do not use for real lending or KYC decisions without an independent audit.
